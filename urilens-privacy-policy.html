Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Information We Collect

URI Lens is a utility application for inspecting URIs and testing Android deep links. The app does not require an account for its core functionality.

URI Lens may process technical and usage information through third-party services such as Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. This may include information about app usage, device and app information, diagnostic information, and crash information.

URIs and deep links that you enter or process in the app may contain information such as URLs, paths, query parameters, identifiers, or other data. URI Lens processes this information locally on your device and does not intentionally send the URI contents or query parameters to our servers or Firebase services.

2. URI and Deep Link Processing

URI Lens uses Android's built-in URI, Intent, and Package Manager functionality to analyze URIs and determine whether installed applications can handle a deep link.

The app may inspect information such as the URI scheme, host, port, path, query, fragment, and query parameters. When testing a deep link, Android may provide information about applications registered to handle the requested Intent.

This processing is performed locally on your device. URI Lens does not upload your URIs, deep links, query parameters, or inspected application information to a developer-controlled server.

3. Firebase Analytics

URI Lens uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to understand general app usage and improve the application.

Firebase Analytics may automatically collect information such as app interactions, usage information, device information, app version, operating system information, and other technical information. Firebase may also use identifiers associated with the app or device as permitted by its service configuration.

URI Lens does not intentionally send the contents of URIs, deep links, query parameters, clipboard contents, or locally stored URI history to Firebase Analytics.

Firebase Analytics processes information according to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

4. Firebase Crashlytics

URI Lens uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google, to identify, diagnose, and improve the stability of the application.

Crashlytics may collect information related to application crashes and technical diagnostics. This may include crash reports, stack traces, device information, operating system information, app version, and other diagnostic information necessary to investigate application stability.

URI Lens does not intentionally include the contents of URIs, deep links, query parameters, clipboard contents, or URI history in Crashlytics crash reports.

Crashlytics processes information according to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

5. Local History

If you use the history feature, URI Lens may store previously inspected or tested URIs locally on your device so that you can access them again.

This history is stored locally and is not transmitted to our servers, Firebase Analytics, or Firebase Crashlytics.

You can delete individual history entries or clear the stored history using the controls provided in the app.

Because URIs may contain sensitive information depending on how they are used, you should avoid saving confidential information in URI history and should use the clear-history functionality when appropriate.

6. Clipboard Access

URI Lens may provide clipboard features that allow you to paste a URI into the app or copy URI information to the clipboard.

Clipboard content is accessed only when required for a user-initiated clipboard operation. URI Lens does not intentionally upload clipboard content to our servers or send clipboard content to Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics.

7. Sharing and Opening Links

URI Lens may use Android's standard sharing and Intent mechanisms when you choose to share a URI or open a link.

When you choose to open or share content, Android and the selected third-party application may process that information according to their respective privacy policies. URI Lens does not control how another application handles information you choose to send to it.

8. Advertising

The current version of URI Lens does not use advertising services to collect information or display personalized advertisements.

If advertising services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the applicable data processing and advertising practices.

9. Third-Party Services

URI Lens uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics — for understanding general app usage and improving the application.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — for crash reporting and application stability diagnostics.

These services are provided by Google and may process technical, diagnostic, and usage information as described above.

URI Lens does not use a developer-controlled backend or require users to create an account for its core functionality.

10. Internet and Network Access

URI Lens uses network access for applicable third-party services such as Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.

The core URI inspection and deep-link testing functionality is performed locally on your device.

URI Lens does not intentionally transmit your inspected URIs, deep links, query parameters, clipboard contents, or local URI history to our servers or Firebase services.

11. How Information Is Used

Information processed by URI Lens and its third-party services may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the application.
  • Understand general application usage.
  • Improve application functionality and user experience.
  • Identify and diagnose crashes and technical problems.
  • Improve application stability and performance.
  • Respond to support requests.

12. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Technical, diagnostic, and usage information may be processed by Google through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics as necessary to provide analytics and crash-reporting services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or operation of the application.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

URI Lens does not maintain your URI history or other personal content on developer-controlled servers.

Locally stored history remains on your device until you delete it through the app or remove the app and its associated application data using Android's standard controls.

Information processed by Firebase services is retained and managed according to the applicable Firebase service configuration and Google's policies.

14. Children's Privacy

URI Lens is a general-purpose utility application and is not specifically directed to children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the app.

15. Security

URI Lens is designed to process URI information locally on the user's device rather than transmitting URI contents to developer-controlled servers.

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled through the application and its associated services. However, no electronic device, operating system, network, or storage mechanism can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's functionality, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements change.

The latest version of this Privacy Policy will be made available on this page, with the updated date shown at the top.
